# Facet

A quiet puzzle. A beautiful breaking point. Seven kinds of cut stone lie on your bench, waiting for the move that turns a small fracture into a glittering cascade. Take hold of a gem, carry it onto a neighbor, and release to make a line of three or more.

Every clear leaves pressure behind. Nearby stones grow hairline cracks, then spreading fractures, until they become flawed. Clear beside a flawed stone and it shatters too, earning double points and carrying the break into its neighbors. Build pressure patiently, then find the seam that lets it all go.

Longer lines leave something precious behind. Four stones create a brilliant that bursts through its surroundings; crossed lines create a star that cuts across an entire row and column. Five or more make a prism: exchange it with a stone to release every gem of that kind. Bring two prisms together to clear the whole bench.

Falling stones can start the next reaction, with each step raising your multiplier. Reach the level target, admire your strongest move, and take a fresh board into the next level. Keep finding possibilities: your round lasts until no legal swap remains.
